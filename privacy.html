Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: possible has no analytics and no account. Here is exactly what leaves your PC.

The app

Licence check. When you activate and each time the app starts, it sends your licence key to Gumroad so they can confirm the key is valid and count how many PCs use it.

Network readouts. The ping figure on the home screen is measured against 1.1.1.1. The DNS test sends ordinary name lookups for www.epicgames.com to public resolvers (Cloudflare, Google, Quad9 and others) and to the resolver you use now.

Everything else stays on your PC. Tweak snapshots and logs are stored locally, in C:\ProgramData\possibletweaks and %LOCALAPPDATA%\possibletweaks. We do not receive them.

Buying

Payments are handled by Gumroad. They collect your name, email, payment details and billing country under their own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details so we can help you.

This website

It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. The Buy buttons take you to Gumroad.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to support email. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you.
